# Reading one word at a time

This reader shows text **one word at a time**, always in the same spot, so your eyes never have to move. The red letter marks the point to focus on.

Tap the word (or press space) to pause. When paused, the sentence you are in appears underneath, so you can catch up on anything you missed.

## Pacing

Short words go by fast. Longer words, numbers like 1,234,567, and `code` linger a bit longer. Commas add a small pause; full stops add a longer one. A new paragraph adds the longest pause of all.

Use the slider, or the up and down arrow keys, to change the speed. Most people are comfortable somewhere between 250 and 450 words per minute. Push it higher once the rhythm feels natural.

## What you can paste

- Plain text, including very long articles.
- Markdown, with headings, *emphasis*, lists and [links](https://example.com).
- A Slack message copied straight from the app. Names, timestamps and reactions are stripped.
- A link. The page is fetched and turned into readable text.

That is all there is to it. Happy reading!
